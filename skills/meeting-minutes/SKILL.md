## Meeting Minutes Format

Use the following structure when writing meeting minutes:

```
# Meeting Minutes

**Meeting:** [Meeting name / project]
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Time:** [HH:MM] – [HH:MM]
**Location / Platform:** [Room or video link]
**Facilitator:** [Name]
**Note-taker:** [Name]

## Attendees
- [Name, Role]
- [Name, Role]

## Apologies / Absent
- [Name]

---

## Agenda Items

### 1. [Agenda Item Title]
**Discussion:**
[Summary of key points raised]

**Decision:**
[What was decided, if any]

**Action Items:**
| # | Action | Owner | Due Date |
|---|--------|-------|----------|
| 1 | [Action] | [Name] | [YYYY-MM-DD] |

---

### 2. [Next Agenda Item]
...

---

## Summary of All Action Items
| # | Action | Owner | Due Date |
|---|--------|-------|----------|
| 1 | ... | ... | ... |

## Next Meeting
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Time:** [HH:MM]
**Agenda preview:** [Brief notes]
```

### Writing Guidelines
- Keep discussion summaries factual and concise (3–5 sentences per item).
- Use passive voice for decisions: "It was agreed that…" not "Bob said…".
- Capture action items immediately after each agenda item.
- Distribute minutes within 24 hours of the meeting.
