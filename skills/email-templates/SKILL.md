## Email Templates

### Leave Request

```
Subject: Leave Request — [Your Name] [Start Date] to [End Date]

Hi [Manager Name],

I would like to request leave from [Start Date] to [End Date] (inclusive).

Reason: [Brief reason, e.g., personal / medical / vacation]

I will ensure all outstanding tasks are handed over before my leave begins.

Please let me know if you need any additional information.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

---

### Meeting Invitation

```
Subject: Meeting Invitation — [Topic] on [Date]

Hi [Recipient Name(s)],

I'd like to invite you to a meeting to discuss [topic].

Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Time: [HH:MM] – [HH:MM]
Location / Link: [Room or video link]
Agenda:
  1. [Item 1]
  2. [Item 2]
  3. AOB

Please confirm your attendance or suggest an alternative time if needed.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

### Weekly Status Report

```
Subject: Weekly Report — [Your Name] — Week of [YYYY-MM-DD]

Hi [Manager / Team],

Here is my status update for the week:

**Completed this week:**
- [Task 1]
- [Task 2]

**In progress:**
- [Task 3] — [brief status note]

**Planned for next week:**
- [Task 4]
- [Task 5]

**Blockers / Risks:**
- [Any issues or none]

Best,
[Your Name]
```
